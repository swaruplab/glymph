Actual: two passes of 54 libraries in 1.88 GPU-hours each (median 101 s, 3.5 GB RAM, 7.0 GB GPU memory per library); scoring of seven tools 54 tasks of 3 to 12 min on 4 CPUs (five rerun after a wrong data link); about 18 GB of outputs on HPC3.

Result in short (decisions.tsv has the numbers): far ahead of CellBender 0.4.0 on the mixtures never used in development (cross-species removal 88.6% against 83.4%, per-cell error 0.019 against 0.047, two runs within 0.01 points), level with it on kidney (error 0.090 against 0.097), 21 times faster. Two faults keep 0.4.0rc1 from being the version to stand on: a runaway fit on bd_demo_pbmc (17% of counts removed, 5% in 0.3.2) and 139 over-stripped cells on the held-out xenograft xeno_noHSC_2 (143 over all mixtures against 9 for 0.3.2 and 4,206 for CellBender). The first went back to development (round M7).
